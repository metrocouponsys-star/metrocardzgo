## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the quiet authority and tactile prestige of an exclusive private members' club under pristine daytime light. It draws primarily from contemporary luxury minimalism infused with subtle tactile realism—evoking fine warm vellum stationery, hand-pressed heavy cardstock, brushed brass hardware, and guilloché-engraved access passes. 

The aesthetic serves high-net-worth urbanites, culinary patrons, and private club members. The interface must communicate exclusivity without visual clutter, pairing expansive warm-pearl negative space with sharp, obsidian ink-like contrast and refined metallic brass details. Elements feel deliberate, architectural, and heirloom-quality.

## Colors

The palette establishes an interplay between radiant pearl surfaces, deep midnight inks, and brushed golden finishes:

- **Primary (`#C59B27`)**: Antique brushed gold. Reserved for high-value focal indicators, active selection boundaries, member tiers, and verified insignia.
- **Secondary (`#1E293B`)**: Rich slate navy. Delivers structural grounding, dark micro-accents, and primary interactive buttons that require strong visual anchor points against light pearl surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`)**: Champagne gold sheen. Used for subtle linear highlights, badges, delicate divider lines, and metallic badge gradients.
- **Neutral Surface Palette**:
  - `Canvas / Base`: `#F8F9FA` (Warm Pearl Linen)
  - `Card / Surface Raised`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Bright Pearl)
  - `Surface Recessed / Track`: `#F3F4F6` (Muted Warm Stone)
  - `Border Trims`: `#E5E7EB` (Subtle boundary) and `#E2E8F0` (Structural dividing lines)
- **Text Hierarchy**:
  - Primary text: `#111827` (Deep Obsidian Ink) for maximum daylight legibility.
  - Secondary text: `#4B5563` (Treated Graphite) for contextual metadata and subheadings.
  - Tertiary / Disabled text: `#9CA3AF` (Muted Alabaster Slate).

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites avant-garde architectural character with pristine, neutral legibility:

- **Headings (Syne)**: Expressive geometric contours that evoke editorial magazines and luxury private house identities. Large letterforms leverage distinctive proportional flares that reflect light and form.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (Outfit)**: Clean, geometric, and spacious. Used for uppercase category tags, member status pills, and numeric data labels with tracking widened to emulate bespoke luxury engraving.
- **Body & Continuous Text (Inter)**: Rational, highly legible, and neutral, balancing the expressiveness of the display faces for effortless readability in bright daylight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column 4-grid unit structure on mobile.

- **Grid Dimensions**: Desktop uses a max-width container of `1280px` with `2rem` gutters and `3.5rem` outer canvas margins to retain breathing room. Mobile devices utilize `1rem` gutters with `1.25rem` margins.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Governed by an 8pt base grid with a 4pt micro-step. Generous vertical breathing room (`space-xl` and above) separates card clusters, framing each metropolitan club benefit or digital pass as an isolated artifact of luxury.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy utilizes warm ambient diffusion rather than cold dropshadows:

- **Ambient Warm Shadows**: Shadows avoid cold grays. They use an ultra-diffuse warm pigment: `rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.04)` combined with a soft grounding base of `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)` with blur radii ranging between `16px` and `32px`.
- **Prestige Insets**: Key membership passes feature a fine inner hairline highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`) combined with a razor-thin perimeter stroke (`#E5E7EB`).
- **Surface Elevation Levels**:
  - `Level 0 (Canvas)`: Flat `#F8F9FA`.
  - `Level 1 (Card & Module)`: `#FFFFFF`, `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.03)`, border: `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
  - `Level 2 (Floating Passes / Hover)`: `#FFFFFF`, `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.08)`, border: `1px solid #C59B27`.
  - `Level 3 (Modals / Overlays)`: Pure `#FFFFFF` backplate surrounded by a warm pearl scrim (`rgba(248, 249, 250, 0.85)` with `12px` backdrop blur).

## Shapes

The design system employs a structured, tailored curvature that balances modern sharpness with refined hand-held ergonomics:

- **Standard Elements (Tokens: Level 2)**: Buttons, cards, and input fields carry an 8px (`0.5rem`) base corner radius.
- **Large Membership Passes & Modals**: Utilize 16px (`1rem`) to resemble premium laminated titanium and acrylic cards.
- **Status Tags & Action Badges**: Employ pill-shaped full-radius capsules for contrast against the architectural geometry of member cards.
- **Borders**: Micro-fine hairline widths (1px) remain uniform across cards, tabs, and input states to preserve clean structural definition.

## Components

### 1. Membership Cards & Passes
- **Structure**: Rendered with an authentic credit card / privilege pass aspect ratio (1.586:1). White pearl layered over subtle warm pearl background with micro-etched guilloché watermark styling.
- **Accents**: 1px brushed antique gold border (`#C59B27`) or dual metallic corner brackets. Member tier rendered in `label-caps` gold lettering.
- **Embossing**: Cardholder names and card numbers set in `Outfit` with high tracking and subtle vertical shadow to mimic debossed foil.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary (Obsidian Prestige)**: `#1E293B` solid background, pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), with an antique gold border hover transition (`#C59B27`).
- **Secondary (Champagne Accent)**: Transparent background, 1.5px solid `#C59B27` border, `#1E293B` text. Hover fills surface with `#C59B27` at 8% opacity.
- **Tertiary (Ghost Link)**: Text in `#1E293B` accompanied by an understated 12px gold arrow icon with 4px trailing gap.

### 3. Chips & Privilege Badges
- **Style**: Compact capsule pills. Background in `#F3F4F6` with 1px border in `#E5E7EB`. Text in `label-caps` Outfit uppercase (`#1E293B`).
- **Active / Tier Status (e.g., VIP, Founder)**: Surface tint in soft gold wash (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.1)`), border in `#C59B27`, and copy in deep antique gold (`#B8860B`).

### 4. Input Fields
- **Container**: Minimalist pearl container (`#FFFFFF`), height of 48px, subtle 1px border in `#E2E8F0`. 
- **Typography**: Placeholder text in `#9CA3AF`, user input in `#111827` using `Inter` 14px.
- **Focus State**: Border transitions to `#C59B27` paired with an ambient gold outer glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.15)`).

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Unchecked**: 1.5px boundary in `#E2E8F0` on `#FFFFFF` ground.
- **Checked**: Solid navy fill (`#1E293B`) adorned with a sharp champagne gold (`#D4AF37`) checkmark or centered inner brass dot.

### 6. Lists & Perks Catalog
- **Structure**: Alternating row items separated by 1px hairline rules in `#E5E7EB`.
- **Perk Meta**: Venue and benefit details grouped with left-aligned obsidian title in `Syne` and right-aligned redemption action in gold `Outfit` caps.
