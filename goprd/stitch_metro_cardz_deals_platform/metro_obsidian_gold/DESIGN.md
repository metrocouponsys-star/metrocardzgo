## Brand & Style

This design system translates the weight, tactile presence, and quiet prestige of a bespoke physical NFC gold card into a mobile-first digital experience. The aesthetic merges understated luxury minimalism with modern high-contrast tactile elements: deep obsidian tiers create architectural depth, while radiant champagne-gold accents direct intent without visual noise.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Restrained Exclusivity:** The interface feels like an invite-only concierge terminal—uncluttered, disciplined, and decisively composed.
- **Physical Heritage:** Digital components reflect material finishes: brushed metallic gradients, specular hairline gold borders, and velvety matte charcoal surfaces.
- **Precision Utility:** High-density data, deal metrics, and compliance partner classifications are rendered with architectural order and micro-typographic clarity.

### Target Audience & Emotional Arc
Tailored for discerning urban professionals and luxury experience seekers. The interface instills a sense of elevated access, institutional trust, and fluid interaction via single-thumb ergonomics designed for high-frequency mobile interaction.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for pure OLED blacks and deep obsidian depth, illuminated by warm auric metallurgy. Contrast ratios meet or exceed WCAG AAA standards for critical transactional content.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0D0F12`):** Primary viewport backdrop; minimizes battery consumption and grounds floating elements.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#14171F`):** Standard container background for cards, bottom sheets, and modules.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1C212B`):** Elevated elements, modal panels, segment controls, and active states.
- **Surface Highlight (`#222936`):** Press states, hovered chips, and nested sub-cards.

### Metallics & Accents
- **Primary Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Primary action buttons, primary branding, active indicators, and high-tier iconography.
- **Champagne Amber Glow (`#E5C158`):** Glow halos, key value badges, and focus rings.
- **Deep Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Time-sensitive tags, expiring deal highlights, and intermediate warnings.
- **Specular Gold Tint (`#FFF3D6`):** Text contrast inside filled metallic buttons, subtle radiant gradients, and card edge highlights.

### Borders & Dividing Lines
- **Hairline Obsidian Border (`#2A303C`):** Structural framework, list dividers, and secondary component contours.
- **Auric Hairline Border (`#3F3722`):** Luxury card outlines, selected states, and merchant verification seals.

### Typography & Neutrals
- **Pristine Contrast (`#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`):** Primary headlines, offer valuations, and key figures.
- **Muted Steel (`#9CA3AF`):** Secondary meta text, standard captions, and inactive tab icons.
- **Faded Charcoal (`#6B7280`):** Disabled states, disclaimers, and non-interactive metadata.

### Compliance Badge Tones
- **`PUBLIC_LINK`:** Slate outline (`#374151`), muted steel typography (`#9CA3AF`), transparent base.
- **`AFFILIATE`:** Deep bronze base (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`), warm gold typography (`#E5C158`), hairline gold border (`#3F3722`).
- **`AUTHORISED_PARTNER`:** Deep emerald night base (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), mint emerald typography (`#34D399`), subtle boundary (`rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.25)`).
- **`DIRECT_MERCHANT`:** Obsidian gold fill (`#1C212B`), dual micro-border gold (`#D4AF37`), gold text (`#FFF3D6`), authenticated shield emblem.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional tension between editorial display forms and technical micro-precision:

- **Headings (Syne):** Sculptural, wide-stance, and commanding. Used for editorial hero statements, tier titles, card values, and merchant names to preserve the tactile personality of card embossment.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern geometric grotesk engineered for digital screens. Delivers open counters and high legibility across dense deal descriptions and numeric disclosures.
- **Micro Badge Standard (`badge-micro` / `label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with tracking between `+0.06em` and `+0.08em`. This counters dark-mode halation and keeps small legal badges readable on compact OLED screens.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout prioritizing single-thumb ergonomics across touch targets and reachable screen zones:

### Screen Adaptation
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Primary controls, redemption actions, and NFC triggers sit anchored in the bottom 40% thumb zone. Bottom navigation sits above system home indicators.
- **Tablet (768px–1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Cards reflow into paired horizontal modules.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column centered layout constrained to a maximum content width of `1200px` with `2rem` (32px) gutters and `3rem` margins.

### Vertical Rhythm & Touch Targets
- All primary interactive elements (buttons, inputs, filters, list triggers) adhere to a **strict minimum touch height of 48px**, with 56px preferred for primary conversion triggers.
- Micro-spacing (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px) controls tight icon-to-label alignment, while macro-spacing (`space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px) demarcates tier transitions and content blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects generic grey drop-shadows in favor of tonal obsidian surface tiers, specular edge lighting, and champagne ambient glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Floor):** Pure `#0D0F12`. Non-interactive backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Background `#14171F` with a continuous 1px stroke of `#2A303C`. Zero ambient drop shadow; structural separation occurs entirely via border contrast.
- **Level 2 (Active/Floating Panels):** Background `#1C212B`, hairline metallic highlight border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`), elevated by a deep shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Bottom Drawers):** Background `#1C212B` with a dual border effect: 1px inner border of `rgba(255, 243, 214, 0.08)` and outer ring of `#0D0F12`. Casts `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

### Auric Ambient Glow
Interactive or featured physical-tier elements utilize a champagne luminescent glow:
- **Hero & Featured Trigger:** `0 0 20px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.
- **NFC Ready State:** Animated breathing border with an ambient auric field: `0 0 32px 2px rgba(229, 193, 88, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp, high-end metallic tailoring with comfortable touch ergonomics via consistent geometric radii:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Standard compliance badges, chips, nested image media, text input fields, and compact toolbars.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px):** Deal cards, modal bottom sheets, balance overview modules, and featured experience cards.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Full-screen takeovers, digital NFC membership card replicas, and floating overlay action sheets.
- **Full Radius (Pill / 9999px):** Primary interactive CTA buttons, quick-filter pills, and single-character status pins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Auric Button:** Height 52px (touch accessible). Fill: linear gradient from `#E5C158` to `#D4AF37`. Typography: `body-semibold` in obsidian `#0D0F12`. Full pill radius (`9999px`). Active state: scale down to 0.98 with subtle inset shadow.
- **Secondary Charcoal Button:** Height 52px. Fill: `#1C212B`. Border: 1px `#2A303C`. Typography: `#F8FAFC`. Subtle gold border transition on focus/press (`#3F3722`).
- **Tertiary Ghost Button:** Height 48px. Fill: transparent. Typography: `#D4AF37`. Underline indicator appears on active press only.

### Compliance & Partner Badges
Must strictly retain consistent height (24px) with uppercase `badge-micro` typography:
- **`PUBLIC_LINK`:** Background `rgba(42, 48, 60, 0.4)`, border 1px solid `#2A303C`, text `#9CA3AF`.
- **`AFFILIATE`:** Background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`, border 1px solid `#3F3722`, text `#E5C158`.
- **`AUTHORISED_PARTNER`:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`, border 1px solid `rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.3)`, text `#34D399`.
- **`DIRECT_MERCHANT`:** Background `#1C212B`, border 1px solid `#D4AF37`, text `#FFF3D6`. Includes a 12px polished gold star/shield glyph.

### Cards (Experiences & Deals)
- **Standard Card:** Background `#14171F`, 1px border `#2A303C`, 16px corner radius. Padding 16px. Features a 16:9 media wrapper with an obsidian overlay gradient fading to `#14171F` at the bottom.
- **Featured Card:** Background `#1C212B`, 1px border `#3F3722`, glowing edge on scroll into view. The top right corner reserves a dedicated anchor for compliance badges.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Standard height: 36px (enclosed within 48px touch bounding box).
- Unselected: Fill `#14171F`, border 1px `#2A303C`, text `#9CA3AF`.
- Selected: Fill `#1C212B`, border 1px `#D4AF37`, text `#FFF3D6`, with a 4px auric active dot.

### Input Fields
- Height: 52px. Fill `#14171F`, border 1px solid `#2A303C`, radius 8px. Text `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder text `#6B7280`.
- Focus state: Border jumps to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Bounding touch target: 48px × 48px. Visible indicator: 20px × 20px.
- Inactive: Border 1.5px `#2A303C`, background transparent.
- Active: Background `#D4AF37`, border `#D4AF37`, checkmark icon in obsidian `#0D0F12`.

### List Items
- Minimum height 64px. Background `#14171F` with a 1px bottom border `#2A303C`.
- Left-aligned icon/avatar container with `#1C212B` fill and 1px `#2A303C` border. Right chevron rendered in `#9CA3AF`.

### Physical NFC Card Mimic (Hero Identity Component)
- Aspect ratio 1.586:1 (standard credit card geometry).
- Visual finish: Radial obsidian brushed gradient from `#1C212B` to `#0D0F12`. Outer edge highlighted with an auric border (`#D4AF37`).
- Embedded NFC pulse icon, embossed metallic chip asset placeholder, masked member serial number in monospace styling, and metallic gold foil logo signature.
